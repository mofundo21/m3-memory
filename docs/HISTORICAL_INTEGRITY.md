# Historical Integrity Policy

Contributor submissions are immutable historical source records.

## Source Record Rules

- Original submissions must never be silently rewritten.
- Corrections belong in separate review records.
- Yeriel notes belong in separate review records.
- AI interpretation belongs in separate AI observation records.
- AI-generated prose is never historical evidence.
- Conflicts must be flagged, not silently resolved.
- Preserve timestamps and provenance.
- Never delete historical records casually.

## Layer Separation

Keep these layers distinct:

- Contributor memory
- Yeriel memory and notes
- AI observation
- Confirmed evidence
- Story draft

Do not merge these layers in the database, UI, exports, or prompts.

## Story Drafting

Narrative drafts may only use reviewed material. A draft must identify its inputs and cannot promote its own output to historical fact or approved book material.

Final authority is Yeriel.
